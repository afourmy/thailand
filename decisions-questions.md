## 1. Line 122, แท่งเทียน (was: 'candlestick')

To answer your question: no, เทียน does not mean wax (wax is ขี้ผึ้ง). เทียน means candle, and แท่ง is the word/classifier for a bar or stick (like แท่งช็อกโกแลต, a chocolate bar). So แท่งเทียน is literally "a stick of candle", i.e. the candle object itself. My "(stick of wax)" parenthesis was just a clumsy way to describe the physical object, forget it.

The open question was which sense you encountered, because แท่งเทียน has two common uses:
- (a) the candle itself, emphasizing its stick shape. Proposed gloss: 'a candle, a stick of candle'
- (b) trading/finance: กราฟแท่งเทียน, a candlestick chart. Proposed gloss: 'candlestick (in a candlestick chart)'
